Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What information is handled

This plugin is currently a skills-only workflow. It processes questionnaire text or files and company evidence that a user chooses to provide in the conversation. Such material may contain business information and, when a user includes it, personal information related to the security review.

Purpose

The information is used to perform the requested questionnaire review: extracting questions, mapping evidence, drafting supported responses, and identifying evidence gaps or items requiring human confirmation.

Recipients

The plugin does not operate a separate customer database, analytics service, advertising system, or external integration in this version. The plugin developer does not receive a separate copy of the user's supplied documents through an independent backend. Information remains subject to the service and account controls through which the workflow is used.

Retention

The plugin itself has no persistent storage layer and does not independently retain uploaded questionnaires or evidence after the interaction. Any retention by the host service is governed by that service's applicable account, plan, settings, and privacy controls.

User controls

Users should provide only information necessary for the review and should redact unnecessary personal information, passwords, API keys, private keys, credentials, and other secrets. Users may choose what documents to provide and may stop using the workflow at any time.

Future changes

If a future version adds persistent storage, analytics, external integrations, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those practices are introduced.

Waitlist

The optional product waitlist is hosted through GitHub Issues. Information you choose to submit there, such as your role, questionnaire frequency, feature needs, and pricing feedback, is submitted to GitHub and is visible according to the repository’s settings. Do not submit confidential company information or secrets.

Support

Questions can be raised through the public GitHub issue tracker.
