Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What information is handled

This plugin is currently a skills-only workflow. It processes the questionnaire text/files and company evidence that a user chooses to provide in the conversation. Such material may contain business information and, where the user includes it, personal information related to the security review.

Purpose

The information is used only to perform the requested questionnaire review: extracting questions, mapping evidence, drafting supported responses, and identifying evidence gaps or items that require human confirmation.

Recipients

The plugin does not operate a separate customer database, analytics service, advertising system, or external integration in this version. Content provided through the ChatGPT experience is processed according to the applicable platform terms and privacy policies. The plugin developer does not claim separate access to the user's private conversation or uploaded documents beyond what the platform makes available for the interaction.

Retention

The plugin itself does not maintain a persistent storage layer or independently retain uploaded questionnaire or evidence files after the interaction. Retention inside the host platform is governed by the applicable account, plan, settings, and platform privacy controls rather than by this plugin.

User controls

Users should provide only information necessary for the review and should redact unnecessary personal information, passwords, API keys, private keys, and other secrets. Users can choose not to provide a document or can stop using the workflow at any time.

Future changes

If a future version adds persistent storage, analytics, external integrations, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those practices are introduced.

Support

Questions can be raised through the public GitHub issue tracker.
