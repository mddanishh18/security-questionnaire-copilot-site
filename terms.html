Terms of Use

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Security Questionnaire Review is an evidence-first drafting workflow for cybersecurity and vendor-security questionnaire preparation.

No certification or legal advice

The workflow is not a certification service, audit opinion, legal service, or guarantee that an organization meets any security, privacy, or compliance requirement.

User responsibility

Users are responsible for reviewing generated responses, validating evidence, and deciding what information to submit to customers or third parties. Generated text must not replace an authorized security, legal, privacy, or compliance review.

Prohibited submissions

Do not provide passwords, API keys, private keys, payment-card information, government identifiers, protected health information, or other unnecessary restricted data. Do not ask the workflow to invent controls, certifications, audit results, or security claims.

Current limitations

The current version does not provide persistent storage, tenant isolation, external integrations, or guaranteed modification of original questionnaire files.

Questions can be raised through the public GitHub issue tracker.
